KatrinaStudio

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

KatrinaStudio is a private productivity application designed to manage studio orders, clients, payments, materials, photographs, exhibitions, and related business information.

Data Storage

KatrinaStudio stores its business data locally on the user's iPhone.

The app does not use a KatrinaStudio cloud backend and does not automatically upload or synchronize its business database with an external server.

Locally stored information may include:

  • client information;
  • order information;
  • payments and expenses;
  • photographs and attachments;
  • inventory and material information;
  • exhibition and studio workflow information.

Data Collection

KatrinaStudio does not collect user or business data for the developer.

The app does not use advertising networks, third-party analytics, tracking technologies, or behavioral profiling.

KatrinaStudio does not sell personal information.

Speech Recognition

Speech recognition is used only when the user explicitly starts the dictation feature.

KatrinaStudio uses Apple's speech recognition technologies. If on-device speech recognition is unavailable, audio may be processed by Apple services in accordance with Apple's privacy practices.

The KatrinaStudio business database is not automatically transmitted to Apple as part of this feature.

Backups

The user can manually create a KatrinaStudio backup.

Backup files are created only at the user's request. The user chooses where the exported backup is stored using system-provided iOS functionality.

KatrinaStudio does not upload backups to a developer-operated server.

Data Retention and Deletion

Business information remains under the control of the user on the user's device.

Individual records may be deleted using KatrinaStudio where the relevant function is available. Application data may also be removed through the operating system.

Security

KatrinaStudio uses the security and data-protection mechanisms provided by iOS and stores its application data within its designated application containers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if KatrinaStudio's functionality or data-processing practices change.

The current version will remain available at this web address.
