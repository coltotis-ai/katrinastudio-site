KatrinaStudio

Private studio management for iPhone.

KatrinaStudio is a private, single-user app for managing studio orders, clients, payments, materials, photographs and exhibitions.

Privacy Policy Support

Local-first

Business data is stored locally on the user's iPhone. KatrinaStudio does not require a developer-operated backend for normal use.

Private by design

No advertising, behavioral tracking or third-party analytics are used by the app.
